# 05 — AI Safety and Risk Review

**Source:** Professor — pre-review email, prompt #5
**Focus:** Hallucination, privacy, data leakage, user harm, bias, misuse, overreliance, transparency.

## Prompt

> You are an AI safety reviewer. Evaluate the following AI startup for risks related to hallucination, privacy, data leakage, user harm, bias, misuse, overreliance, and lack of transparency. Identify the most important risks and propose concrete safeguards. Be specific about what should be added to the product, the documentation, and the user interface.

## When to use

Before the demo and before publishing the business plan. For Resell Copilot specifically, expect this prompt to surface: hallucinated brand identifications (impacts authenticity claims), price suggestions that mislead novice sellers, scraped image rights, EU data-handling under GDPR, and the limits of "verify" UX — all of which need explicit copy in the product and slide deck.
